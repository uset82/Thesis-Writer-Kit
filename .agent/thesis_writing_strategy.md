# Thesis Writing Strategy: Authentic & High-Complexity Style

## 🎯 Objective
Produce text that is **authentic, deeply analytical, and human-written**, avoiding detection as AI-generated.

## 🛡️ "Stealth" Strategy: Humanizing the Text

The key to bypassing detection is **specificity** and **structural complexity**.

### 1. Specificity Over Generalization
* **AI Tendency**: "AI technologies are improving efficiency..."
* **Human/Academic**: "Equinor’s deployment of the **Omnia Prevent** platform... drove **$120 million** in value."
* **Rule**: Never make a general statement without attaching a specific *Name, Date, Number, or Location*.

### 2. Syntactic Variation
* **Rule**: Use complex sentence structures, dependent clauses, and varied transitions. Avoid "In addition," "Moreover."
* **Burstiness**: Alternate between <10 word sentences and >30 word sentences.

### 3. Critical Hedging & Nuance
* **Rule**: Use academic hedging ("suggests," "indicates") but take a firm stance on the *evidence*.

### 4. Tone & Voice
* **Technique**: Use rhetorical questions ("Why does this matter?") and direct address ("Consider the case of...").
* **Voice**: Preference for active voice ("Equinor decided to...") over passive ("It was decided...").

### 5. Stealth Profile (Humanizer Replication)
* **Colloquialisms**: "put... to work" instead of "deployed".
* **Simple Numbers**: "a fifth" instead of "20%"; "$330m" instead of "$330 million".
* **Structure Quirks**: Strategic passive voice for results; occasional object-first construction.

---

## 🚫 "AI Markers" to Avoid (Red Flags)

1. **The "In Conclusion" Paragraph**: Avoid it. Synthesize and look forward instead.
2. **"Crucial/Vital/Important"**: Don't label it important; *show* why it matters.
3. **Moralizing**: "We must ensure..." -> Stick to policy analysis.
4. **Lists**: Avoid excessive bullet points. Use prose.
5. **Repetitive Transitions**: Don't start every paragraph with a transition word.

---

## 🛠️ Humanizer Guidelines (for Rewriting)

When rewriting text to sound human:
1. **Focus on Clarity**: Remove fluff and marketing hype.
2. **Be Direct**: Simplify grammar, avoid AI clichés ("dive into", "unleash").
3. **Natural Tone**: It's okay to start sentences with "And" or "But".
4. **Context**: Ensure the emotional nuance and logic remain intact.
